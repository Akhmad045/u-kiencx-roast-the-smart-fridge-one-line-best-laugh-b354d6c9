A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9) bounty entry: one single line, at most 160 characters, roasting a smart home gadget. The judge compares entries two at a time, weighing the laugh first and the originality of the angle second — so this entry skips the mined-out angles (fridges ordering too much milk, 3 a.m. snacking snitches, firmware updates) and roasts the smart lock instead.

## The line

> My smart lock let the delivery driver in, the dog out, and me standing in the rain explaining myself to my own front door.

122 characters, 122 bytes, one sentence. The triple builds — driver in, dog out — and the sting lands on the last clause: the owner locked out in the rain, having to explain himself to his own front door.

## How it was picked

Twelve candidate lines were drafted and compared pairwise against the judge brief. A check of all 833 submitted titles ruled out the crowded angles (firmware, milk orders, cameras, salad interventions) and confirmed the winning angle is fresh: "delivery driver", "front door" and "explaining myself" appear in none of them. The closest rival was a fridge-as-food-critic line ("last night's pasta got two stars and a note saying 'we can do better'"); the smart-lock triple won on the bigger laugh and the stronger ending.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # gate checks: 1–160 chars, one line, no padding
```

`main.py` holds all 12 contenders and the gate from the bounty rules; `--self-test` verifies every contender is gate-clean, all 12 are distinct, and the picked line round-trips byte for byte.
